Last updated November 18, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Button Box Labs, doing business as Button Box Pets ("we," "us," or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when creating an account, building pet care profiles, dictating care notes, or submitting sitter check-ins.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you join our waitlist, register on the Services, create or update a pet profile, share or interact with a sitter care link, or contact us. The personal information we collect may include:

  • Account & Contact Details: names, email addresses, account credentials (passwords managed securely via our authentication provider), and notification preferences.
  • Pet Care Profiles & Media: pet names, species/breeds, feeding schedules, medications, behavioral notes, user-uploaded pet photos, and sitter check-in photos or notes.
  • Third-Party Care & Emergency Contacts Entered by Owners: names, phone numbers, and clinic details for veterinarians, emergency animal hospitals, backup family contacts, and pet sitters that an owner enters into a pet's care profile. If you enter another person's contact information into Button Box Pets, you represent that you have their permission to share those details for pet-care coordination and emergency purposes.
  • Optional Voice Dictation Audio: if you choose to use our voice-to-care dictation feature, your browser requests temporary microphone access to record your spoken care instructions for transcription and structuring.

Sensitive Information. We do not collect or process sensitive personal information (such as government IDs, financial account numbers, biometric identifiers, health records about humans, or precise GPS coordinates).

Social Media Login Data. If we offer the option to register or log in using a third-party account (such as Google or Apple), we receive basic profile details from that provider as described in "HOW DO WE HANDLE YOUR SOCIAL LOGINS?" below.

Browser Permissions & Web Notifications. When using our web app or PWA, your browser may ask for permission to upload photos from your device gallery/camera, access your microphone when you tap the voice-dictation button, or send optional web push notifications (for example, when a sitter views a link or posts a care update). You can revoke these permissions at any time in your browser or device settings.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

Information automatically collected

In Short: Some technical information—such as your IP address, browser type, and access timestamps—is collected automatically when you visit our Services or open a sitter care link.

We automatically collect certain technical information when you visit, use, or navigate the Services (including when a pet sitter opens a shared care link without logging in). This information is needed to maintain the security, reliability, and operation of our Services, prevent abuse, and show pet owners when their shared care link was accessed. The information we collect includes:

  • Log and Usage Data. Service-related diagnostic, security, and usage logs collected automatically by our servers, including IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages or sitter links accessed, timestamps of sitter link visits, and error reports.
  • Approximate Location Derived from IP. General city/region-level location inferred from your IP address by our hosting and security infrastructure (such as Cloudflare) for security, DDoS protection, and regional compliance. We do not collect precise GPS location data.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. HOW TEMPORARY SITTER LINKS & SHARED CARE PROFILES WORK

In Short: Sitter share links provide temporary access to the specific pet care instructions you choose to share, and allow recipients to submit care check-in notes and photos during the active link window. Owners may also choose view-only access.

A core feature of Button Box Pets is the ability for pet owners to generate a unique, temporary web link for a pet's care profile and share it with a pet sitter, dog walker, or family caregiver.

  • No Login Required for Link Recipients: Anyone who possesses an active sitter link and any required access code can view all information included in that shared pet profile—including feeding instructions, medications, behavioral notes, pet photos, and any veterinarian or emergency contact names and phone numbers you added—without creating a Button Box Pets account.
  • Owner Responsibility When Sharing: You are responsible for sharing sitter links only with trusted caregivers and revoking links if a link is forwarded or shared unintentionally.
  • Sitter Visit Logs & Check-In Updates: When a sitter opens a shared link, we automatically log the visit (including timestamp, IP address, and browser/device type) so the pet owner knows the care guide was accessed. If the sitter submits a care check-in or uploads a status photo through the link, that update and photo are stored and displayed to the pet owner.
  • Expiration & Revocation: Sitter links automatically stop working once their set expiration time passes or immediately when the pet owner revokes or deletes the link.

4. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

In Short: We share information only with third-party service providers who help us run Button Box Pets, with caregivers whom you send a sitter link, or when required by law. We never sell your personal information.

We may share or disclose your personal information in the following specific situations:

  • Infrastructure, Hosting & Storage Service Providers. We rely on trusted third-party vendors to host, store, secure, and deliver the Services pursuant to written data-processing agreements that restrict them from using your data for their own purposes. Specifically, we use Supabase (authentication and PostgreSQL database), Cloudflare (pet and sitter photo storage, DNS, CDN, and security protection), and Vercel (web application and serverless API hosting). All core application data and uploaded media are hosted and processed in the United States.
  • AI Service Providers (Opt-In Only). When you actively use our optional AI voice-to-care dictation or pet-care organization tools, your audio or text input is transmitted to OpenAI and/or Google Cloud AI solely to perform the requested transcription and formatting, as detailed in Section 6.
  • Caregivers & Sitters via Share Links. Information on a pet care profile is made accessible to any recipient to whom you send an active temporary sitter link.
  • Legal Obligations & Business Transfers. We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable law, subpoena, or court order, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets (in which case we will notify affected users).

We use essential cookies and local browser storage (such as Supabase authentication session tokens and security tokens from Cloudflare) to keep you logged in, remember basic app preferences, prevent abuse, and maintain site functionality. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies, cross-site ad pixels, or Google Analytics trackers.

6. DO WE OFFER ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-BASED PRODUCTS?

In Short: Yes. We offer optional AI-powered voice-to-care dictation and routine-organization tools powered by OpenAI and Google Cloud AI.

As part of our Services, we offer optional features powered by artificial intelligence (collectively, "AI Products"). These tools are designed to save pet owners time when creating or updating pet care profiles.

What Our AI Features Do & What Data They Process

  • Voice-to-Care Dictation & Routine Structuring: When you tap the microphone button to dictate your pet's routine, your recorded voice audio and resulting transcript—along with any care notes you submit for organization—are processed to extract and format feeding schedules, medication instructions, behavioral notes, and emergency contacts into structured profile fields.
  • Pet-Care Organization Assistance: If you use AI tools to summarize, clarify, or organize pet-care instructions, the text you enter is processed to generate a clear, sitter-friendly care summary. (AI outputs are organizational aids only and do not constitute veterinary medical advice.)

AI Service Providers, Audio Deletion & Model Training Safeguards

We provide these AI features through third-party API providers, specifically OpenAI and Google Cloud AI. Your audio and text inputs are transmitted securely over HTTPS to these providers solely to generate your requested transcription or structured care notes. Button Box Pets does not store raw voice audio recordings—once transcription completes, the audio file is immediately discarded from our servers. Under our API configurations and provider data-usage policies, your inputs and outputs are not used to train public or generalized AI models (though AI API providers may briefly retain encrypted API logs for up to 30 days strictly for automated abuse and safety monitoring).

How to Opt Out

Use of AI features in Button Box Pets is completely optional. Manual typing is always available as the default way to create and edit pet profiles. You can opt out at any time simply by not clicking the voice-dictation or AI-organize buttons, or by disabling AI features in your account settings or emailing privacy@buttonboxpets.com.

8. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We keep your account and pet profiles only while your account remains active, and we delete removed photos, deleted pet profiles, and expired sitter links immediately from active systems (with logs and backups purged within 30 days).

  • Active User Accounts: We retain your account details and pet profiles for as long as you maintain an active Button Box Pets account (or remain subscribed to our waitlist). If you delete your account, we delete your personal information and pet records from our active databases.
  • Deleted Pet Profiles & Photos: When you delete a pet profile, a sitter check-in update, or an uploaded photo inside the app, both the database record and the stored image file are removed from active storage and are no longer accessible to you or any sitter.
  • Expired or Revoked Sitter Links: When a temporary sitter link expires or is revoked by the owner, the link immediately stops working and can no longer be used to view the pet profile.
  • Security Logs & Database Backups: Server access and security logs (including sitter link visit timestamps and IP addresses) are retained for up to 30 days for security and troubleshooting. Encrypted database backups are isolated and automatically overwritten on a 30-day rolling schedule.

9. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

In Short: We protect your information using HTTPS encryption in transit, strict database access controls, and unguessable sitter link tokens.

We have implemented reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your personal information—including TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, strict database and storage access controls so owners can access only their own pet records, and unguessable cryptographic tokens for temporary sitter links. However, no electronic transmission over the Internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. In the event of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will investigate promptly and notify you and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.
